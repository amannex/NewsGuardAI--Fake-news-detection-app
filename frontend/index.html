The Fake News Epidemic

In an era of rapid information sharing, distinguishing fact from fiction is a modern-day challenge. Fake news not only misinforms but can manipulate elections, economies, and public health.

Our solution utilizes Explainable AI (XAI). Instead of accepting a black-box model's answer, we generate human-readable explanations using LIME (Local Interpretable Model-Agnostic Explanations), so you can understand exactly which words influenced the prediction.

💡

Why XAI matters:

  • 🔍 Transparency: See inside deep learning
  • ⚖️ Trust: Builds user confidence
  • 🛡️ Security: Exposes model biases

How It Works

The journey from raw text to explainable intelligence.

1
Input

You paste a news article or headline into our classification engine.

2
Processing

We tokenize, pad, and analyze the sequence using an advanced BiLSTM Neural Network.

3
Prediction

The system predicts realism: is the content REAL or FAKE along with a confidence score.

4
Explanation

Finally, using LIME, we highlight the exact terms that pushed the model toward its conclusion.
